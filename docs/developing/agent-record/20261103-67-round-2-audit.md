## What was done

### Repeat in stats, view and action passes

`a_repeat_runs_in_stats_view_and_action_passes_on_the_device` in `crates/henad-graph/src/tests/gpu_repeat.rs` reads a grid of 8 by 8 cells whose `u32` lane `x` holds `cell % 11`, so three cells in eleven ask for more iterations than `max = 8`.
Each pass holds a Repeat whose count is the cell's `x`, compiled by `compile_shaders` and run once on the device and once through `HostEvaluator::run_pass`.

- **Stats.** The body sums `k`, carries a hash chain from the cell's index and counts its iterations. Three `stat.count_where` nodes count the cells whose sum is 6, whose chain ends odd and whose count was clamped to 8. The device counts the tallies in the reduce shader and the host finishes them, as the engine does, and every statistic equals the host's run. The first and the third also equal their closed forms, the cells with `x = 4` and with `x >= 8`.
- **View.** A `count` accumulator counts the even iterations, with `when` false at `x = 3`, and `view.color` colours the cell by that count from a nine-colour palette. Every texel equals the host's colour index, and the indices equal `ceil(min(x, 8) / 2)`, 0 where `when` is false.
- **Action.** A `carry` holds `3 * prev + k` from the cell's index, and the pass writes it to `x`. The device's lane equals the host's and the fold computed in the test.

A plant that ran one iteration too few in the emitter's loop (`k + 1u < total`) failed the test at its first comparison, the clamped count, 0 on the device against 16 on the host.
`color_index` in `gpu_cases.rs` became `pub(super)` for it.

### The float Repeat gate on Metal

The probe reproduces the failing test outside wgpu and changes one thing, Metal's math mode.

1. A temporary test wrote the composed WGSL of the loop (`CARRIED` in a tick pass) and of its copies (`chained()`), their uniform blocks and wgpu's output of lane `y`, and the host's.
2. naga 30, the version wgpu 30 uses, translated each to MSL 3.2 with the bounds-check policy, loop bounding and buffer-sizes slot wgpu-hal sets, and buffer slots 0 to 2 for `x`, `y` and the uniform block.
3. A Swift program compiled each MSL with `MTLCompileOptions` holding `preserveInvariance = true`, as wgpu-hal sets it, under five settings, then dispatched one 16 by 16 threadgroup over the 8 by 8 grid and read both lanes back.

| Setting | Loop and copies, cells of `y` that differ | Loop equals host | Copies equal host | Equals wgpu's words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Metal's default, as wgpu compiles | 32 of 64 | no | no | yes, loop and copies |
| `mathMode = .fast` | 32 of 64 | no | no | yes |
| `mathMode = .relaxed` | 32 of 64 | no | no | yes |
| `mathMode = .safe` | 0 | yes | yes | no |
| `fastMathEnabled = false` | 0 | yes | yes | no |

Lane `x`, the integer half, agreed under every setting.
The default reproduces wgpu's words exactly, and safe math makes the loop, the copies and the host agree bit for bit.
wgpu-hal 30 sets only the language version and `preserveInvariance` on `MTLCompileOptions` (`src/metal/device.rs`), and exposes no math mode.
The cause in record #66 holds.

Two committed changes follow from it, and neither touches the gate.
`a_float_repeat_body_replays_on_the_device` runs the float body twice on one device, loop and copies each, and asserts equal words: the replay half of the first way forward below, which held before and is now tested.
The ignored test's note and `agents/gpu-traps.md` record the confirmation.

The gate stays failed.
The three ways forward of record #66 stand, and each needs the maintainer.

- **Read the gate as integer bodies plus replay.** Both are tested and pass. This changes the plan's wording.
- **Safe math on Metal.** wgpu 30 has no option, so this is a `[patch]` of wgpu-hal or an upstream feature. Stock GPU ports would compile under it too, so stock and graph stay equal, but every Metal float result moves, the stock models' included, and the cost of safe math is unmeasured.
- **Break contraction in the emitter.** An opaque step between a multiply and an add costs every float node a few instructions, parts a graph from a stock port that writes the fused form, and still leaves the reassociation of adds and the reciprocal of a division to the compiler.

### Accessible bounds on the canvas

egui 0.36 writes a widget's AccessKit bounds from its rectangle in the coordinates of its layer, and applies no layer transform.
egui-snarl draws every node on a layer of its own (`LayerId::new(order, canvas id)`) with a pan and zoom transform, so each widget on a node had the bounds it would have at scale 1 and no pan.
`show_canvas` now ends with `place_accessible_nodes`, which reads the layer's transform and, for each widget on that layer with an AccessKit node this pass, maps the node's bounds through it.
It does nothing when AccessKit is off or the layer has no transform.

`accessible_bounds_of_a_node_follow_the_canvas_transform` in `crates/henad-app/src/tests/graph_canvas.rs` frames n12 of the SIR starter on a headless context with AccessKit on, and asserts that the one node reading Subtract lies within the node's drawn rectangle.
With the call removed it fails, the label at 8, 192 and the node drawn at 857, 476.
In the running app, through the egui MCP server, the Subtract label of n12 now reads at 981, 495, 10 by 3.4 points, inside the canvas at its small zoom, where it was drawn.
