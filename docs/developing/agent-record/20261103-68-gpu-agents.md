# GPU agents from graph files

> A graph file of agents now compiles to WGSL and runs on the GPU agent engine, from the CLI's `--model-file` and from anything that builds a `ModelFile` entry.
> Library 1 holds the kinds of A2's row, 114 kinds in all, with host and WGSL forms and known-answer cases that run on the device.
> A contraction probe found that the device fuses `field * cut + reward` alike in the stock ants shader's one expression and in the generated shader's two `let`s.
> `gpu_ants.toml` then equals the stock `gpu_ants` bit for bit every tick on one device, in every lane, both pheromone layers, the colours and the three statistics, across a press of Reset colony.
> The ants starter equals the stock ants at tick 0, under the cross-engine gate and in its decay, and both boids graphs meet the boids gate and track `gpu_boids`.
> The plan's Repeat tests hold over agents, a `grid.fold` nested in a Repeat and a Repeat in a `grid.fold` included.
> A radius fold past 65536 visits faults with its pass, node and line, and the device runs another model afterwards.
> Every agents graph hashes its schema as the stock models do under their ids, and the testing kit passes every agents entry.

### Step 3: the oracles and the gates

#### The contraction probe

The stock `step.wgsl` computes a deposit as `max(best, field[...] * cut + reward)`, one expression.
The generated `lay` shader writes `let n43_out = n41_value * n42_out; let n44_out = n43_out + n35_value;` and then the `max`.
Record #66 found that Metal compiles with fast math, so the device may fuse a multiply and an add.
`the_device_rounds_the_deposit_alike_in_both_forms` (henad-models, `src/tests/graph_ants_parity.rs`) runs both forms over 65536 inputs in the ranges an ants run meets, the stock cuts and rewards among them.
On the Apple M4 Pro both forms returned the same bits for every input.
Of the 6216 inputs where a fused multiply-add and a rounded product and sum differ, the device returned the fused result 6216 times and the rounded one never.
The device contracts, and a `let` boundary does not stop it, so the two shaders round alike.
The test asserts the first equality alone and prints the counts.
It ran on one GPU. The plan asks for two, and no second GPU was at hand.

#### The ants starter

`ants.toml` draws keyed words where the stock ants draw from a stream, so it is held where no draw decides.

- **Tick 0 on the device.** `the_ants_starter_starts_as_gpu_ants_on_the_device` builds both at two worlds and two seeds and compares positions, the packed fields, both layers and the sites.
- **The cross-engine gate.** `the_ants_starter_equals_the_stock_ants_under_the_gate` plants the twelve ants and the tie-free field of `tests/consistency_ants.rs` and runs its four ticks with momentum and random action off. Every ant's cell, last step, load and reward, and every cell of both layers, equal the CPU `ants` bit for bit, at four seeds. `planted_entry` builds the entry from `AgentProgram::spec` with `PlantedHooks`, which wrap the graph's `AgentHooks` and replace the seeded words of the named buffers. Note that this equality is not guaranteed: the CPU rounds the deposit's product and sum apart and the device fuses them. Over these 48 deposits each fused result rounded to the same bits, since a reward of 1 or more dominates the sum.
- **Decay.** `cells_without_an_ant_decay_by_exactly_the_evaporation_rate_on_the_device` runs the check of `consistency_ants.rs` on the device: 400 ants in a world of 100, evaporation 0.99, and every cell no ant stands on equals its value times 0.99, floored at `LOW_PHEROMONE`, bit for bit over 40 ticks.
- **The other checks of `consistency_ants.rs`**: on the lattice, in the world, off every obstacle, a load of 0 or 1, and Deliveries never decreasing, over 40 ticks.

#### The Repeat tests over agents

`src/tests/graph_agent_repeat.rs` places agent `i` at the centre of cell `(i % 40, (i / 40) % 30)` of a 40 by 30 world, so each expected value has a closed form, and runs each graph on the device and through the host evaluator.

- Known answers: zero iterations and `when` false return the initial value, a count of 100 runs `max` 8 times, a count of `index % 6` and an inner count of the outer `k + 1` give their sums.
- A hash chain of five `rng.hash` links equals its five hand-chained copies and the host bit for bit, and equals `pcg_hash` applied five times to the index.
- Two iterations of one `rand.bits` differ for every agent, two runs at one seed are equal, another seed differs, and the device equals the host.
- A `grid.fold` nested in a Repeat, centred `k` columns to the right, and a Repeat nested in a `grid.fold` sum a static field of small integers to their closed forms, bit for bit on both.
- The random walk replays at one seed, its last step is `step_size` long, its mean squared step over a tick is `sub_steps * step_size^2` within five standard errors, and with no steps it stands still.
- A Repeat of 16384 iterations, within the pass limit, is refused by the submission budget at a million agents, by `shortfalls` and by a build.

The float half of the Repeat gate was not run over agents, since record #66 showed it fails on Metal for grids and the cause does not depend on the topology.

### Review of A2

A review of the A2 commits confirmed twelve findings, and a fourth step fixed them in six commits, `5c31cd9` to `cc9ccb1`, and the commit holding this section.
Each behaviour fix has a test that fails without it, run against the tree with the fix taken out.

- **A counter total read per element zeroed every statistic of its pass (high).** A stats pass whose per-element block read a value computed from `stat.counter` made that value a uniform member.
  The host evaluated it with no counters and faulted, and the hooks replaced the whole Reduce block with zeros, `henad_agents` and `henad_groups_x` included.
  A counter total is now ready only after the elements, as a reducer's result is (`NodeDef::is_ready_after_elements`).
  The host runs it in the post block, and the checker refuses an element read of it, naming a counter total (`a_counter_total_is_read_after_the_elements_alone`).
  `AgentHooks::run_params_bytes` returns a `Result`, so a prelude fault fails a build with its pass and node and refuses a press, and no block is zeroed silently (`a_step_block_the_hooks_cannot_compute_fails_the_build`, `an_action_block_the_hooks_cannot_compute_refuses_the_press`).
  The reviewer's `ants_counter.toml` is now refused at line 1487 with the pass and node.
- **The field update read the wrong cell above layer 0 (medium).** The update ran `finish` with `henad_index = layer * cells + cell`.
  It now runs one invocation per slot `henad_slot`, binds `henad_index` to `henad_slot % cells`, and writes the field at the slot.
  A draw in a `finish` reads the slot as its element word, so two layers of a cell draw apart.
  A `finish` that reads its own field, which the update writes in place, is refused by the checker at the `field.get` (`check_finishes`), and by the emitter as a fault of the compiler.
  `a_field_update_reads_each_layer_at_its_own_cell` cuts both layers at a wall on the device.
  Without the fix, layer `b` kept its value at the wall.
- **A read of `prev.<acc>` in a radius fold left `replays_exactly` set (low).** `fold_order_matters` now flags any read of a `prev` slot, as it flags `k`, and the catalogue's semantics say so.
  The library lock moved with the semantics.
- **A flat `math.expr` chain overflowed the stack (high).** The parser counted depth for parentheses and unary operators alone.
  Each operand of a chain and each component read now counts a level of `MAX_DEPTH` (64), which bounds the tree that `build` and its drop recurse through.
  `a_long_flat_expression_is_refused_within_a_small_stack` checks chains of 20000 operands on a 256 KiB thread.
  Without the fix the thread overflowed and the test process aborted.
  The reviewer's `small.toml` is refused at `ulimit -s 1024` with the expression's line.
- **The submission budget did not bound a radius fold's walk (high).** `henad::hash` gained `walk_cells` and `MAX_WALK_CELLS` (65536).
  A walk whose bounds cover more cells faults before it starts, with a second fault code per fold and its own located message, on the device and on the host (`WalkEnd::Cells`).
  `AgentHooks::step_cost` adds each step's walk to its cost in `Demand`, at the parameters: the cells the radius covers over the index, plus the agents a uniform spread puts there, at most every agent and the visit cap, times the body's instruction count plus `VISIT_COST` (8).
  The reviewer's `sparse_walk.toml` at 60000 agents in a world of 2048, which took 10.4 s for one step and exited cleanly, now faults in 0.12 s with `the pass 'crowd', node n4 on line 41: Agents in radius would walk more than 65536 cells of the neighbour index around one agent, and the run stops`.
  `a_radius_fold_over_the_whole_world_is_refused_or_faults_with_its_place` holds both the budget's refusal over coarse cells and the fault over fine ones, then runs stock `gpu_boids`.
  Plan end-to-end item 5 now holds for a world-covering radius.
- **A run that read no stats ignored the fault word (medium).** `GpuSimState::read_fault` (provided, default nothing) reads the counters back when `AgentHooks::reports_faults` holds, and `stepping::wait_for` calls it after its wait.
  `run_steps`, `run_steps_acting` and the benchmark's last wait use it.
  The reviewer's `crowd_all.toml` in a world of 1000 now exits 1 with the located fault where it printed a rep and a summary (`a_recorded_fault_ends_a_run_that_reads_no_stats`).
  In its own world of 10 the budget refuses it before it builds.
- **The normalised document met the 1 MiB file limit (medium).** `compile_parts` reads it through `GraphFile::from_normalised`, which skips the two size limits of `parse`.
  The inlined node limit bounds that text instead.
- **A small file could normalise to hundreds of MB (medium).** `body_size` counts a `math.expr` as the nodes it expands into, and `cost::check` now runs before normalisation, so a file past `MAX_NODES_INLINED` is refused before anything is inlined.
  `expansions_count_toward_the_inlined_limit_and_no_text_limit_refuses_them` holds a file under 64 KiB whose normalised text passes 1 MiB to a successful compile, and a larger one to the limit.

#### Decisions of the review

- **Counter totals are refused per element** rather than read on the device.
  A total is a `u64` the shader cannot hold, and a value read before the readback would change with the order of the stats passes.
- **A field's `finish` may not read its own field**, refused in the checker, rather than merging into a separate output.
  A read at another cell or another layer races with the invocation writing it, and the merged value already arrives as `value`.
- **Two caps per radius fold.** A cell cap of 65536 beside the visit cap keeps the visit message as it was, and a walk past it faults before its first cell, so a world-covering radius costs one step.
- **The walk charge assumes a uniform spread**, at most the visit cap per agent.
  Charging the cap for every agent would refuse the stock-default boids at 50000 agents.
  Agents that gather visit more than the charge, and the device caps bound each walk then.
- **`VISIT_COST` is 8 units**, the old flat `body + 8` read as the cost of one visit's gather.
  It is not calibrated: the one measurement above suggests a visit costs more.
- **The crowd fault test moved to a world of 1000.** In a world of 10 the new charge refuses the crowd at build, since it charges every agent a full walk although only agent 0 walks.
  The assertions are unchanged.
- **The budget test was left failing**, with the evidence above, since the charge it contradicts is the fix the review asked for.

## Issues found & future directions

- **A second GPU.** The probe, the `gpu_ants.toml` oracle and the ants gate ran on Metal alone. On a backend that does not contract, the stock and generated forms would still round alike, but the ants gate's equality with the CPU could then hold for a different reason, and on one that contracts only the single expression the oracle could fail. Run `graph_ants_parity` on Vulkan or DX12 before calling the probe's gate met.
- **Fast math, again.** The ants gate equals the CPU only because every fused deposit rounded like the unfused one over its 48 deposits. A longer gate, or other rewards, could part them. The three ways forward of record #66 still wait on the maintainer.
- **The faulting corner widens as a flock forms.** The table holds tick 0. A run at a million boids in a world of 2000 and range 200 starts at 55272 visits, 84 % of the cap, and boids gather. The stock port has no cap and slows instead. A cap past 65536, or a fault message that suggests a larger world or a smaller range, are for the maintainer.
- **Small populations pay for passes.** The graph's ants run one step pass more than the stock and a colour pass per snapshot, which costs 60 % to 90 % at 2000 ants and 15 % to 30 % at a million. Fusing `lay` and `walk` into one shader, as the stock fuses deposit and advect, would remove a pass. The compiler could do so when neither pass reads a lane the other writes.
- **The float Repeat gate over agents.** Not run, since the grid version is `#[ignore]`d for fast math and the cause is the same. The random walk on the device stays within 1e-4 of the host, not bit for bit.
- **The interleaved stock gate** passed on a loaded machine that had not cooled. A repeat on an idle, cooled machine would narrow the rounds at 4096 agents.
- **The boids graphs and the budget.** The review's walk charge refuses the boids graphs above about 201000 agents in the default world, and the budget test fails on it.
  The choices are in the review section.
- **The walk charge is uncalibrated.** It assumes a uniform spread and 8 units per visit.
  One loaded measurement suggests a visit costs about four times what the grid calibration implies.
  Measure stock `gpu_boids` at several densities on an idle machine before trusting it.
- **The grid hooks still zero a faulting block.** `GridHooks::pass_params_bytes` returns zeros for a prelude the host cannot evaluate, as the agent hooks did.
  No grid kind faults there on a checked file, and the trait method is required, so the review left it.
