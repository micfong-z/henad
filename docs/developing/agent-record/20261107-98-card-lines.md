# Card lines

> S19 makes the Card pane the place where lines are written.
> Under each card of cells, "+ Look around", "+ Chance", "+ Yes or no", "+ Calculate" and "+ Change" open menus with the final editor's headings and grey patterns, and each item adds a line at the end of the card, whose first blank opens by itself.
> A blank or a chip opens a picker that lists only what fits its place: the results of the lines above with "line N: [sentence]" under each, the cell's properties, the parameters with "New parameter...", the states with "any state" first for an old state, and a Number box that refuses a chance outside 0 to 1 and a negative rate.
> Every choice is one `SheetEdit` edit and one step of Undo, and the focus then moves to the next blank, an empty state blank and the upper number of a range opening by themselves.
> Game of Life and the chance SIR are built from Blank by accessible names alone, and after the alignment of plan section 4.4 each equals its sheet in canonical text and in lowered code.

## What was done

**Places on sentence parts (henad-graph).**
`Sentences::placed_parts` returns each part of a line's sentence with the `PartPlace` an edit of it sets: a `PlaceTarget` (a slot by its slot path such as `condition.left`, an item of a list by the list's slot path and its index, or a field of the line or of a phrase, `FieldPlace`) and the `Blank` the place shows while empty.
`Parts`, the one builder of the card's parts and the writer's comments, now carries places beside the parts, and `Parts::under` composes a phrase's relative places under the slot that holds it.
`line_parts` keeps its signature and returns the parts alone.
`SentencePart::ForEach` is the words "for each" once on (`FOR_EACH_WORDS`), placed at `tries` with their count.
A Count's comparison after "whose" wants a property on its left and, while that is empty or a States property, a state on its right, so a new Count reads "whose [property] is [state]".
An empty Calculate line reads "[number] [operation] [number]" with both values and the select placed, `binary_formula` splits a formula of one operation, and `Sentences::formula_operand` returns the chip of one value of a formula.
`rates::chance_note` writes the note of a chance with "for each" (the chance at 1 itself, at 2 by the number rule against 2p, or words first for a chance that is a result, D106), and `rates::count_as_draw` names a Count held as a chance's or a rate's value.

**The Card pane (henad-app).**
- `card_pane(ui, sheet, past_start)` draws the cards and runs the one edit collected in the frame through `SheetEditor::edit`.
  A world of cells with no tick card shows the empty card with the add buttons, and its first line adds the card.
- Every chip, blank, select, "always", "for each" and optional "for each ..." blank takes a widget id from its line and place (`CardState::control_id`), so a later frame can open its picker or give it the focus.
  The condition arrow (D104) follows the first condition word of a Become or Set, named "Line 2: change the kind of condition".
  "+" after a list is named "Line 3: add another value".
- `picker.rs` builds a picker from what a place wants (`picker_for`, `number_picker`, `choose_when`) and turns a choice into commands (`apply_choice`): a Count's state also sets its property and a new property clears a state of another, a list item edits the whole list and an emptied list turns blank, an old state of another property sets the line's property, and a form of condition is `ChooseCondition`.
  "Choose when" puts "otherwise" in place of "always" when the line, made always on a copy of the sheet, would read "otherwise".
- A picker opens on a click and stays open on a second one, closes on a choice, on Escape (the focus back on its anchor) or on a click outside, and scrolls past twelve items.
  Items are named by their first line, and "line N: [sentence]", values and units and the condition explanations sit under them in grey.
  The Number box comes first and takes the focus, except for the value left of a comparison and a "for each" count, and shows a typed number as typed when its picker reopens.
  "2, 3" in a box of one number adds "For a list such as 2, 3, choose is one of the numbers or is none of the numbers first." after "Type one number, such as 3 or 0.25.".
- The add menus show the final headings and grey patterns, painted outside the accessibility tree, with the rate items left out until S25 and "+ Change" alone on a button's card.
- "Line 2 actions", always drawn, holds "Rename line 2" on a result line and "Delete line 2".
  Rename opens "Line 2: new name" under the line, and a taken name shows "Another result is already called X." with the field focused again.
- Notes under a line follow the wording table's order: the skip notes, the one-side offer with its button, the check's other notes, the count used as a chance or rate with "Use a chance for each infected neighbour", and the grey chance-for-each note.
  The one-side offer waits while `CardState::filling` holds the line (D103).
- `NumberUse::Rate` refuses a negative rate, and the New parameter dialog of a rate starts at 0.1 in 0 to 1 with "per [unit]" as its Unit.

## Deviations

- **henad-graph changes.** The plan places S19 in `card_pane.rs` and `picker.rs`, but the card could not edit a part without knowing its slot.
  `placed_parts` and its types, `SentencePart::ForEach`, `binary_formula`, `formula_operand`, `chance_note` and `count_as_draw` were added in henad-graph, where the sentence and the notes live.
  `line_parts` and every sentence as plain text are unchanged, except two sentences no starter holds: a Count whose comparison is empty now reads "whose ... is ..." with a property and a state blank, and an empty Calculate line "... ... ..." in place of "...".
- **Calculate values in the pane.** A Calculate line stores one formula string, which cannot hold one value of two.
  The values chosen before both are known stay in `CardState::drafts`, shown as chips, and the formula is written once both are chosen.
  A draft is no step of Undo and goes on Undo, Redo or another sheet.
- **A percent in a Calculate value.** The formula string holds numbers only, so a percent typed as a Calculate line's value is written as its decimal, and its chip reads "0.3" where D110 shows "30%" as typed.
- **A topic file of its own.** S26 grows `agents/henad-app-sheet.md` in parallel, and the card pane's text would push it near 40k characters.
  The card pane went to a new `agents/henad-app-card.md` with a row in `AGENTS.md`'s table, and `agents/henad-app-sheet.md` keeps a pointer.
- **The chance SIR's id and format.** The id `sir_chance` cannot come from the name "SIR epidemic", so the rebuild names the model "SIR chance" in the New model dialog and renames it in the toolbar, which keeps the id.
  `align_with_starter` now also copies a parameter's `format`, which no control sets, as it copies `step` and `means`.
  The sheet is read from a copy in `crates/henad-app/src/tests/sheets/`, since `include_str!` stays inside its crate.
- **No rate items.** "Become, at a rate", "At a rate" and "at rate ... per ..." wait for S25, as the gate's section 11 says.
  A rate blank in a starter still opens a picker that refuses a negative rate.
- **The plan's "once for each" note.** F9's "Each one is a separate chance." is replaced by the gate's chance-for-each note of 10.11, as section 11 of the gate says.
- **A short picker does not scroll.** egui's scroll area inside a popup lagged a frame behind its content and hid the last item of a short picker, so only a picker of more than twelve items scrolls.
- **The New parameter dialog opens a frame later.** The click that chose "New parameter..." landed on the modal's backdrop and closed it in the same frame.
- **No screenshots.** The screen was locked for the whole check, as recorded above.

## Issues found & future directions

- **The Card pane is narrow.** In a 1600-point window, with the Viewport and Playback tabs beside the Editor, the card pane is about 160 points wide, and a line wraps over three to five rows.
  A wider default for the card, or the Sheet pane collapsing, would help.
- **Focus after a list.** After "2, 3" in a list blank, the focus lands on the first item of the new list, the next control by place order.
  The prototype moves past the list.
- **The one-side mark.** The mark ends when the focus lands outside the line and no picker of it is open, read from egui memory each frame.
  Between the frame a picker closes and the frame the upper number opens, nothing holds the focus, which the test passes through but a slower frame might not.
- **Overlapping nodes in the harness.** A picker covers the card's own chips, and the harness has no tree of parents, so the tests find picker items by bounds and check item order through `picker_for` directly.
- **Calculate drafts.** They are no step of Undo, and a formula of more than one operation, written by hand, is read-only on the card.
  So is a formula inside a slot, such as a chance written by hand as `{ kind = "calculate", formula = ... }`.
- **T1's evidence is incomplete.** Plan S19's acceptance asks for a screenshot of each check, the Viewport changing across two Steps, and the Charts tab's Alive curve falling and settling.
  The screen stayed locked through both checks, so neither has a screenshot, the Viewport was never seen to change (only the tick counter), and the Alive measure was read from the Stats tab and the Charts legend.
  T1 does not count as passed until the merge point repeats it with the screen unlocked, under the lock of plan section 2.4, or the maintainer checks it.
- **Line kinds' help.** The help of each kind of line stays unopened, as in the final editor.
