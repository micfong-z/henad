# Add-up merge

> S15 of the #37 redesign plan builds row D, which the maintainer's answer 10a puts before release, with the power-of-two resolution of decision D49.
> `FieldCombine` gains `Sum { resolution_shift }`, a resolution of 2^-k for k from 0 to 24, written in the file as `combine = "sum"` and `resolution = <number>`.
> The reader refuses any other resolution at its key and names the nearest allowed value, refuses a resolution beside another rule, and requires one beside `sum`.
> On the device a deposit into a sum adds `u32(round(value * 2^k))` with `atomicAdd`, and the field update merges the cell plus the word times 2^-k.
> The host evaluator keeps a sum's deposits as the same `u32` word, rounded with `f32::round_ties_even`.
> A NaN or a negative deposit adds 0, as it already did for the max.
> The checker refuses a deposit whose literal, or whose parameter's maximum, reaches 2^32 times the resolution, and names that bound.
> A million deposits into one cell, ties, NaNs and negatives among them, leave the same word on the device and on the host, and a model of agents that adds up replays bit for bit and merges the host's word.

## What was done

### henad-graph

- **`FieldCombine::Sum { resolution_shift: u8 }`.** `FieldCombine` derives `Copy`, `Eq` and `Hash`, so the variant holds the exponent, never the `f32`. `MAX_RESOLUTION_SHIFT` is 24, `as_str` returns `"sum"`, `resolution()` returns the `f32` 2^-k, `wrap_bound()` returns 2^32 times it, and `FieldCombine::sum(f64)` returns the sum of a resolution or a message naming the nearest allowed value. The power of two is built from its exponent bits, so it is exact for every shift.
- **Reader and writer.** `FieldTable` takes `resolution` as a spanned `f64`. A sum with no resolution is refused at the field, a resolution with `max` or with no `combine` at the key, and a value that is not exactly 2^-k at the key, as "the resolution 0.001 is not a power of two from 1 down to 2^-24. The nearest allowed value is 0.0009765625 (2^-10)". The writer writes `resolution` after `combine` in the writer's number form, `1.52587890625e-5` for 2^-16.
- **Checker.** `check/agents.rs` refuses a deposit into a sum whose `value` is an `f32` literal, or a parameter whose maximum, at or past the wrap bound: "n3 deposits up to 65536.0 into the field 'f', and a cell of that field wraps once it takes 65536.0 in a tick (2^32 times its resolution)".
- **Host evaluator.** `HostLanes::new` makes a sum's deposit column `u32`, and `Operation::Deposit` carries the field's `combine`. The deposit adds `sum_word(max(value, 0), k)`, which is `(value * 2^k).round_ties_even() as u32`, with a wrapping add. `HostLanes::deposits` documents the two column types.
- **WGSL compiler.** `FieldPlan` carries the `combine`. Inside the existing test of the value, a sum's deposit is `atomicAdd(&accum[...], u32(round(henad_deposit_nK * 65536.0f)))`, the scale a literal. The field update of a sum reads `henad_word` and merges `field + f32(henad_word) * 1.52587890625e-5f`. The max's lines are unchanged, so every max model's shaders keep their text.
- **Catalogue.** `field.deposit`'s semantics now describe both rules, the rounding, the wrap and the exact range, and a second case, "a sum in whole resolutions, rounded half to even", deposits 0.5, 0.375, 0.625 and -1 at a resolution of 0.25 and expects the words 4, 2 and 0. `library.lock` changes in `field.deposit`'s line alone, its semantics and cases hashes.

### Deviations from the plan

- **The wrap-bound refusal sits in `check/agents.rs`.** The plan names `check/declarations.rs`, but the bound concerns a deposit's value, which only the per-pass check sees with its node and place. A shift past 24 never reaches the checker, since every `GraphFile` is read through the reader, so `declarations.rs` has no change.
- **What the checker can refuse.** "A deposit sum at the wrap bound is refused by the checker" is read as a deposit whose amount the file fixes, a literal or a parameter's maximum, at or past the bound. A deposit computed at run time cannot be bounded by the checker. Its word wraps at 2^32 as a counter does, and the docs page says so.
- **`FieldCombine` has more than `resolution()`.** `FieldCombine::sum` (the checked constructor the reader uses), `wrap_bound()`, `deposit_scale()` (added at review) and `MAX_RESOLUTION_SHIFT` are public too. The reader builds a sum through `sum`, and the checker reads `wrap_bound`.
- **A sum's host deposit column holds `u32` words.** The plan says the host does "the same with `f32::round_ties_even`". Holding the word, not an `f32` total, keeps the host's wrap and its exactness equal to the device's, and `HostLanes::deposits` now returns `u32` for a sum.

## Issues found & future directions

- **No model adds up yet.** S20 lowers the sheet's "add up" marks to `combine = "sum"` at 2^-16, and S26 offers it in the editor.
- **The merge is exact below 2^24 resolutions per cell.** Above that, `f32(word)` rounds, and WGSL leaves the rounding of an integer to float conversion to the implementation. At 2^-16 that is a total of 256 in one tick, well inside the wrap bound of 65536. A sheet whose marks can pass 256 a tick at 2^-16 would merge within one rounding of the host, not bit for bit.
- **A large single deposit saturates.** A deposit past 2^32 resolutions converts to `u32::MAX` on both sides. The checker catches it only when the amount is a literal or a parameter.
- **NaN under fast math.** The million-deposit test plants NaNs, and Metal's fast math still skips them through the `> 0.0` test on the device used here. A device that folds that comparison under a no-NaN assumption would add a garbage word. `agents/gpu-traps.md` holds the fast-math entry.
- **The host evaluator still has no field update.** Its deposit columns stay unmerged, as for the max. The merge test writes the merge out in the test.
