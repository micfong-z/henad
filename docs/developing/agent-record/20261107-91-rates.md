# Rates

> S21 lowers the rates of a sheet.
> A `[time]` table becomes the hidden first parameter `tick_length`, in the tick's unit, so a sweep varies the tick length of one compiled model with its keys unchanged.
> A rate per unit is `1 - e^(-r dt)`, with a literal factor where its unit is not the tick's, and every value of parameters is a prelude value the host computes, so the SIR tick shader holds no `exp`.
> A rate for each of the cells around reads `e^(-r dt k)` from a power table, and any other count takes a guarded `math.exp` per element.
> Two or more Become lines that leave one state at a rate each draw once, against cumulative shares of `1 - e^(-dt (a_1 + ... + a_k))`, keyed by the highest line.
> `sheet::rates` converts between rates and chances and writes the rate notes of the final wording table, `SheetEdit::ChangeToRate` and `ChangeToChance` keep the chance per tick, and a rate read in another unit than its parameter's warns.
> SIR joins the starters with rates per day and one tick of 1 day, and on the device its epidemic converges as the tick halves while the chance SIR does not, and the SIRD sheet splits its removed cells at 0.5 where the higher-wins lowering gives 0.4134.

## What was done

### The tick length

`lower/world.rs` writes a sheet's `[time]` as the first `[[param]]`: `tick_length`, an `f32` labelled "One tick is (days)", its default the sheet's length, its `min` and `step` a hundredth of it and its `max` 30 times it, `hidden`, and `GraphDocument.time` naming it with the tick's unit (D4).
For the SIR starter that is the `[[param]]` of `sir_rates.toml`, 0.01 to 30 by 0.01.
It is written whether or not a line reads it, so a sheet with a time scale always has its time axis and `time` column.
The id was already reserved in the item namespace.

### A rate

`crates/henad-graph/src/sheet/lower/rates.rs` is new.
A rate phrase or Rate line lowers to `h = r * f * tick_length`, where `f` is the literal ratio of the tick's unit to the rate's in days when it is not 1, then `1 - e^(-h)` and one `rand.chance`.
A rate per week in a model of days takes `f = 1/7`, and a rate per day in a model of hours `1/24`.
With a "for each" over a count of the cells around and a rate the same for every element, the line is `1 - powi(e^(-h), n)`, `max_exponent` 8, 4 or 9, the form of the hand-lowered `sir_rates.toml`.
Any other count, a count of agents or a rate worked out per element included, is `1 - select(n <= 0, 1, e^(-h n))` in `f32`, the guard of the power rule.
The lowered SIR's tick pass holds, for line 2, `math.mul`, `math.neg`, `math.exp`, `math.powi` and `math.sub` before its draw, and for line 3 the same without the power, all of it prelude values.
The refusal of a rate on cells and agents cards and `sentence::NOT_YET_RATE` are gone.

### Competing changes

`CardLowering::groups` holds the card's competing groups less their skipped lines, two lines or more, set before the lines lower.
Each Become of a group lowers at its own turn through `group_line`: its condition with the rate read as yes (the rate found as the condition or as an item of an all of, the When lines around it joined), and its hazard `a = r * f`, times `n` for a "for each", put through `select(gate, a, 0)` when the gate is not always yes.
Its change takes a placeholder condition, so it keeps its place in the row order.
After the group's last line, `finish_group` writes at the highest line's rate site the running sums of the hazards, `t = 1 - e^(-S * tick_length)`, `max(S, 1e-30)` and one `rand.float` up to 1, keyed by the highest line, and at each line's rate site `t * sum_j / max(S, 1e-30)` (or `t` for the last line) and `cmp.lt(u, threshold)` joined with its gate.
The transition takes the first row that holds, so each line takes its share whatever the order of the lines.
For the SIRD sheet with fixed rates that is exactly the node list of `sird_competing.toml`: one add, the tick length, the exponent, the leaving chance, the divisor, the share, one draw and two comparisons.
A line of another kind between two lines of a group keeps the "otherwise" the card shows for it.

`CardRelations::competing_groups` now takes the rate only as the condition or as an item of an all of: a rate inside an any of or a none of keeps its line out of every group, since no share of a hazard is defined there.

### The unit of a rate (D14)

The checker warns at the line when a rate's `rate` is a parameter whose Unit names another unit than the line's `rate_unit` (`edit::unit_in_text`, the first "per [unit]" of the text, D89), in `unit_mismatch`'s words: "Recovery rate is per day on the sheet, but this line reads it per week."
The line's unit governs the run.

### Notes and conversions

`crates/henad-graph/src/sheet/rates.rs` is new and public as `sheet::rates`:

- `chance_from_rate`, `rate_from_chance`, `tick_in` (one tick in a unit, through `TimeUnit::days`), `chance_per_tick`, `mean_stay` and `parameter_defaults`.
- `chance_words`, the number rule of the final wording table (C3): three significant figures, and more until the chance prints differently from `r dt n`.
  `1 - e^(-0.001)` prints as 0.0009995, never 0.001.
- `LONG_TICK_CHANCE` (0.5, D2) and `largest_count_chance`, a `LargestCount` with `is_long` and `words` ("up to 0.909 with 8"), for a fixed rate with a "for each" of a Count over the cells around, or a Calculate of a fixed number times such a Count.
- `rate_note(document, line_id, values)` returns a `RateNote` with `text`, `mean_stay` and `long_tick`, read with the values passed (`parameter_defaults` while no run is loaded, D62).
  The subject follows 10.11 ("an Infected cell becomes Recovered", "a cell takes the new value", "the lines inside line 4 apply to a cell", "the result Recovery happens is yes for a cell"), and Δt prints in the rate's unit with three figures ("0.25 days").
  A fixed rate takes `rate_note` or `rate_for_each_note`, a Calculate of a fixed number times a Count with no "for each" `worked_out_rate_note` (D106), and any other worked-out rate `growing_rate_note` with the Calculate's words.
  The highest line of a competing group takes `competing_note`, with the mean stay over the sum of the rates, and the group's other lines no note.

`SheetEdit::ChangeToRate` and `SheetEdit::ChangeToChance` (T9) turn the typed chance of a Chance line or of a change's condition into the rate `-ln(1 - p) / dt` per the tick's unit, marked as taken from the tick, and back into `1 - e^(-r dt)` with `dt` in the rate's unit.
Both keep "for each" and the line's key, and write the shortest decimal that reads back as the same `f32`, so `1 - e^(-0.1)` becomes 0.1 per day and the same chance again.

### Deviations from the plan

- **The long-tick words print three figures.**
  The plan and the gate's 10.21 quote "up to 0.91 with 8", written before the number rule of round C (C3) gave every chance three significant figures.
  `LargestCount::words` prints "up to 0.909 with 8", as the note's other chances print 0.259 and 0.451.
- **The uniform test's tolerance.**
  The acceptance asks for the host's prelude values within one `f32` step of `1 - e^(-0.1)` and of the table `e^(-0.3 k)`.
  The host computes in `f32`, and `1 - e^(-0.1)` came out two steps from the true value, all of it the rounding of `e^(-0.1)` that the subtraction leaves exposed.
  The test holds `1 - value` within one step of `e^(-0.1)`, exact by Sterbenz's lemma, and the table's base within one step of `e^(-0.3)`.
  The table's powers are `f32` `powi` of the base on the host and in the uniform block alike, and come within 3 steps of the true powers, which the test holds.
  Computing the table in `f64` would change the engine's exact parity for every `math.powi` and the outputs of round 1's chance SIR, outside this milestone.
- **Competing groups exclude a rate in an any of or a none of.**
  S5's rule searched any of and none of too, and the hazard of a line whose rate is one choice of an any of has no share to split.
  The rule now takes the rate as the condition or as an item of an all of, and such a line keeps "otherwise" and draws alone, as `a_rate_in_an_any_of_keeps_otherwise_and_draws_alone` holds.
- **A group's note needs fixed rates in one unit.**
  `competing_note` states one chance, one share per line and one formula over one Δt, which a group with a "for each", a worked-out rate or two units does not have.
  Such a group has no note, and its lines none either.
- **The conversion commands refuse a value that is no typed number.**
  The plan says nothing of a parameter or a result in the chance or rate.
  Converting a parameter would change it for every line that reads it, so `ChangeToRate` and `ChangeToChance` refuse, and S25 can leave the menu item out for such a line.
- **The skip note.**
  The plan's "Not run yet: set one tick on the sheet" is the gate's "Not run yet: set how long one tick is, under World on the sheet", which S5 built and S21 keeps.
- **Where the tests sit.**
  `two_runs_at_one_seed_write_identical_statistics` runs on the device in henad-models, the conservation test on the host in henad-graph beside the chance SIR's, and the `--vary tick_length` sweep in henad-cli's model file tests.
- **The SIRD sheet has a copy in henad-graph.**
  The host tests of the competing thresholds and notes need it, and henad-graph's sources cannot read henad-models' fixtures.
  Its Dead line is `l4` and New outbreak's `l5`, as `every_starter_id_follows_the_minting_rule` holds every test sheet's lines in creation order.
- **The checker's mutation corpus seeds each sheet.**
  Moving SIR into the starters reordered `sheets()`, and the corpus's share of edits that read fell to 1284 of 5160, under the test's quarter.
  Each sheet now draws its edits from the test's seed and the hash of its name, so S20's ants and later sheets leave the others' edits alone, and the share passes.
- **A line inside a Repeat joins no competing group.**
  Added at review.
  S20 makes the condition of a change inside a Repeat an "at any time" accumulator (`any_time`), and `finish_group` replaces a grouped line's condition after that step.
  `CardRelations::group_key` keeps a line with a Repeat around it out of every group, so it draws alone and reads "otherwise", and the S20 merge stays textual.
  A Become inside a Repeat is still refused on this branch, so nothing runs differently before S20.
- **A group's note and the mean stay need the rate alone.**
  Added at review.
  A rate in an all of with more items, or a line inside a When or a Repeat, keeps its note but has no mean stay, and a group with such a line has no note.
  The leave chance and the shares would otherwise be wrong for the cells whose other conditions fail.
- **A third commit.**
  `fix: s21 comment wording [draft]` takes decision and row ids out of source comments and varies the reasons in four of them.

## Issues found & future directions

- **The note and the card.**
  S25 draws `rate_note` under each line, the group note under a group's highest line, the long tick with `LONG_TICK`, the mean stay as the AccessKit description, and "Change to a rate" and "Change to a chance" in the line menu, leaving them out for a line whose value is no typed number and in a button's card.
- **The long tick needs a count.**
  `largest_count_chance` reads only a "for each" or a product with a count of the cells around.
  A rate with no count whose chance per tick passes 0.5 (a rate of 1 per day at one tick of 1 day gives 0.632) gets no long-tick note, and D2 says nothing of that case.
- **A competing group with per-element hazards on the device.**
  The host test splits such a group on 128 by 128 cells, and the device gates exercise only a group of fixed rates.
  The host and the device already step alike for the rates SIR, and a device run of a group with a "for each" would close the gap.
- **A formula names an unnamed line by its id.**
  `rate_note` now names an unnamed Count by its automatic name in the numbers, but the words of a Calculate formula still print "0.3 times l1" for that Count.
  The formula words come from `Sentences`, which S21 does not change.
- **The mean stay with other exits.**
  The mean stay leaves out a rate with more to its condition, but not a state that a chance line or another rate line outside the group also leaves.
- **The power table's precision.**
  `math.powi`'s table is `f32` `powi`, within 3 steps of the true powers at the SIR's base.
  An `f64` table on the host and in the uniform block would bring it to one step, at the price of every recorded run of a `math.powi` model.
- **Agents.**
  A rate on a card of agents lowers through `math.exp` per agent, and no agent starter has a time scale yet, so the mobile SIR of round 1 section 7 would be the first to use it.
