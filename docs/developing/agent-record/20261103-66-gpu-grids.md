# GPU grids from graph files

> A grid graph file now compiles to WGSL and runs on the GPU, from the CLI's `--model-file` and from anything that builds a `ModelFile` entry.
> The grid engine builds from a generalised `GpuGridSpec`, with dual and plain buffer sets, several step passes, multi-pass actions after a host rerun, and the stock models as an adapter.
> `Demand` counts the work of each pass and refuses a submission past a budget measured on an Apple M4 Pro.
> Game of Life, Vote and `gpu_sir_exact.toml` equal their stock models every tick on one device, presses included.
> The SIR starter equals `gpu_sir` at tick 0 and under its rate gates, and meets the SIR bands past them.
> A new fixture, `repeat_trials.toml`, holds `region.repeat` to `1 - (1 - p)^trials`, and the testing kit passes every grid graph entry.
> One Repeat test fails: a float body and its hand-chained copies differ in the last bit on Metal, which wgpu compiles with fast math on.
> A review then found that nothing held a reduce shader's workgroup memory or a pass's uniform block to the device, and that a Repeat body refused values the checker allowed. Both are fixed, with tests that fail without the fixes.

## What was done

### Step 1: the draw core, the spec and the budget

`draw_core` in `draw.rs` is `pcg4d2`: `pcg4d(pcg4d([k, e, t, (i << 4) | (s >> 2)]))[s & 3]`, with `pcg4d` the function of Jarzynski and Olano.
The `rand.*` case words and their lock lines moved with it.

`GpuGridSpec` became lowering section 7.3's generalised spec.
Its buffers fall into a dual set, swapped once at the end of a step, and a plain set read and written in place.
A step is a list of passes, each with a `SideTable` and a cost per invocation, and display and reduce are passes too.
An action holds a list of passes that run after `GridHooks::rerun` and its uploads.
`GpuGridSpec::single_step` and `from_model` reproduce the one orientation the stock shaders assume, so `GpuGridState<M>` is an adapter whose API did not change.
`SideTable`, `SideRow` and `BufferSide` moved to `gpu/sides.rs`, shared by both specs.

`PassBindings` gained its `PassKind` and its work, and `Demand` its host runs.
`Demand::shortfalls` refuses a submission past `SUBMISSION_WORK_BUDGET` (600e9 units) and naming the heaviest pass, and a host run past `HOST_WORK_BUDGET` (4e9 units).
Both come from one measurement on an Apple M4 Pro, which `agents/henad-compute.md` holds: stock `gpu_sir` at 16384 by 16384 ran about 46 steps a second, and the heaviest corpus file there, `gpu_sir_exact.toml`, needs 4.99e11 units a submission.

### Step 2: the compiler and its entries

`henad_graph::wgsl` writes one `GeneratedShader` per role: a step per enabled tick pass, a display shader, a reduce shader and one per action pass.
Each body is the host's compiled `Program` written as WGSL, so the two backends share one schedule.
Values that read no tick are evaluated on the host into the uniform block, and a reduce shader's tallies finish on the host.
A Repeat lowers to a counted `for` loop, a grid count is unrolled over its stencil, and a draw calls `draw_core` from `draw.wgsl`.
`GridProgram::entry` builds a `ModelEntry`, and `ModelFile::entry` and the CLI's `--model-file` run grid graphs through it.
A problem the compiler finds is placed on the node whose text it wrote.
Every known-answer case of scope `Both`, 200 in all, runs on a device through the compiler's own shaders.
`graph_grid_parity.rs` holds Game of Life and Vote to their stock models every tick, Randomise and Clear press by press.

### Step 3: SIR, Repeat and the kit

`graph_sir_parity.rs` holds the two SIR files to stock `gpu_sir` on one device.

- `gpu_sir_exact.toml`, which advances a plain `rng` lane by `pcg_hash` as the stock step does, equals `gpu_sir` every tick in cells, `rng` words and the three counts, at three sizes and seeds, over a 64-step batch, and over three presses of Seed outbreak with steps between them.
- The starter `sir.toml` draws counter-based numbers. It equals `gpu_sir` at tick 0 at three seeds.
- Under each rate gate, an infection rate of 0 or 1 with a recovery rate of 0 or 1, no decision is left to a draw, and the starter equals `gpu_sir` every tick for 40 ticks and at every press.
- Past the gates it meets the bands of `tests/consistency_sir.rs` at that test's size, rates and seed and one more seed: the infection rate per count of infected neighbours within five standard deviations of `1 - (1 - beta)^k` over at least eight buckets, no infection without an infected neighbour, the recovery rate within five standard deviations of `gamma`, and only forward transitions with the population conserved over five ticks.

Planted errors showed the tests bite.
The starter with a von Neumann count failed both its gate test and its bands, and the exact port with an `rng` lane that never advances failed at tick 1.
Two plants that change a comparison's equality case, such as `<` to `<=` against a draw, passed, since a draw of 24 bits never equals the rate exactly.

`repeat_trials.toml` sits in `crates/henad-models/tests/fixtures/graphs/`.
Each tick every cell runs a Repeat of `trials` iterations, each drawing `rand.chance` at `p` into an `or` accumulator, and the cell records whether any draw hit.
Its bound is `max = 4`, the largest whose submission at 16384 by 16384 fits the budget: a bound of 5 costs 38 units a cell a step, which is 6.5e11 units over 64 steps.
`graph_repeat.rs` holds three gates.

- The share of hits on the device is within five standard deviations of `1 - (1 - p)^trials` at eight settings over four ticks at 512 by 512, exactly 0 at `trials = 0` or `p = 0` and exactly 1 at `p = 1`, and each tick draws apart from the last. A plant that turned the accumulator into a `carry` failed it.
- Each device tick equals the host evaluator's `run_pass` of the tick pass, cell for cell, at two seeds over six ticks.
- A copy with `max = 140` checks with no diagnostic and costs under 1000 units a cell, far within the pass limit of 65536. `Demand::shortfalls` admits it at 2048 by 2048 and refuses it at 4096 by 4096 with one line naming its step pass, and `build` at that size fails before the engine allocates or seeds a buffer.

`gpu_repeat.rs` in henad-graph runs the Repeat bodies of `host.rs` in a tick pass, through the step shader on a device and through `run_pass` on the host.
Two iterations of one draw differ at every cell, two runs at one seed are equal, and each draw equals the host's `draw_bits` at its cell, tick and iteration, nested ones in mixed radix included.
The body of `CARRIED` has an integer half, a hash chain, and a float half, `prev * 1.1 + f32(k)`.
The integer half equals five hand-chained copies and the host bit for bit.
The float half does not.
These tests ran Repeat in tick passes only.
The round 2 audit fixes (record #67) added a device test of a Repeat in a stats, a view and an action pass.

### The float half on Metal

The loop and its copies differ in the last bit at some cells on an Apple M4 Pro, and neither equals the host at every cell.
Three probes on the same body place the cause in the device compiler:

| Body | Loop equals copies | Loop equals host | Copies equal host |
|---|---|---|---|
| `prev * 1.1 + k` | no | no | no |
| `prev + 1.1 + k` | no | yes | no |
| `prev * 2.0 + k` | yes | yes | yes |
| `prev / 1.1 + k` | no | no | no |

On the host the loop and the copies agree in every row.
wgpu 30's Metal backend sets only `preserveInvariance` on `MTLCompileOptions`, and Metal's default turns fast math on.
The rows match what fast math allows: a multiply and an add contracted into one rounding, the copies' literal `k` folded into a chain of adds, and a division replaced by a multiply with the reciprocal.
An exact multiply leaves nothing to round, and all three agree.

The stock ports compile under the same options, which is why `gpu_sir_exact.toml` still equals `gpu_sir`: both write `pow(1.0 - rate, f32(count))` and compare it with the same draw.
The test that asserts the float half is `a_float_repeat_body_equals_its_hand_chained_copies_on_the_device`, `#[ignore]`d with the reason, and it fails when run with `--ignored`.
Record #67 confirms the cause: naga's MSL of both shaders, compiled with `MTLMathMode.safe`, equals itself and the host bit for bit, and compiled with Metal's default reproduces wgpu's words exactly.
`agents/gpu-traps.md` holds the trap.

### The review

A review of the eleven commits confirmed nine findings, and one commit, `c9c0462`, fixes them all.
Each behaviour fix has a test that failed with the fix taken out.

- **Workgroup memory (high).** The reduce shader declares `var<workgroup> henad_partial: array<atomic<u32>, N>` with one word a tally, and nothing held N to `max_compute_workgroup_storage_size`. wgpu does not check it. A file of 65 `stat.count_by` nodes over 256 variants built on Metal and reported 8192 for a stat of 4096 cells. `PassBindings` and `GridPassSpec` gained `workgroup_bytes`, the compiler sets four bytes a tally on the reduce pass, and `Demand::shortfalls` refuses a pass past the device's limit, naming it. `raise` leaves that limit at the baseline's 16 KiB, so the refusal holds on the web and on every native device alike. The reviewer's 64-node file, which counted right by luck at 64 KiB, is refused too.
- **Uniform-rate values in a Repeat body (medium).** The checker and the host accepted `rng.lane_seed`, `ctx.press_seed` and a `math.powi` whose base the body computes from parameters, and the emitter refused all three, since it hoisted host values only from a pass's top-level prelude. The host compiler now moves every pure body instruction that reads only constants, parameters and prelude slots to the end of the prelude (`hoist_uniform_body_values`), and the emitter reads it from the uniform block as it reads any prelude value. Draws, stream bits, lane access and effects never move. `Operation::operands` moved from the emitter to `host/compile.rs`, since both now read it.
- **Uniform block size (medium).** No check held a generated uniform block to `max_uniform_buffer_binding_size`, and 400 powi tables failed at `create_bind_group` with no place in the file. `GridPassSpec` and `PassBindings` gained `uniform_bytes`, the compiler sets it from each block's size, and `shortfalls` refuses a pass past the limit.
- **Shader size (low).** A checked file of 2048 grid counts expands to 3.56 MB of WGSL, and the composer's 1 MiB refusal read as a fault of the compiler. The compiler now checks the size before composing and places the problem on the pass whose nodes wrote the most of it. `compose::MAX_FILE_BYTES` became public for that check.
- **Smaller findings.** The ignored float Repeat test cited record #64 for its probes, which are in this record, #66. `henad::gpu` re-exports `PassKind`, `HostWork` and both work budgets, which `Demand::push_pass` and `PassBindings` need. `AGENTS.md` names henad-graph's `src/tests/support.rs` beside henad-compute's as a device kept below the testing kit. Six comment lines past 120 columns are rewrapped, and two error strings with them. The CHANGELOG folds the `GridHooks` changes into the Added entry, since the trait is new in this release.

The reviewer's files ran through the debug CLI after the fix.
The three tally files past 16 KiB that ran (64, 65 and 17 nodes), the 400-table file and the 2048-count file are refused before a build with a line naming the pass, and the 15-count and 560-count files run as before.

### Decisions made on the maintainer's behalf

- **The record is #66.** The task named it #64, which record 64, the round 1 audit, already holds. E1 runs beside this milestone in its own worktree and probably takes #65, so G2 takes #66.
- **`repeat_trials.toml` keeps `max = 4`.** The plan sets the budget above every corpus file at its largest size, and the budget was measured in step 1. The fixture was fitted to the budget, and the budget was left as measured.
- **The trials run in a tick pass, with no init pass.** The device draws them every tick, and the host checks them through `run_pass`. A host init of the same work at 16384 by 16384 would pass the host budget.
- **`Oracle::Trials` has no stock schema.** `graph_corpus.rs` skips it in the schema test and asserts that four files were compared. Two henad-graph corpus tests learnt that a corpus file may have no init pass and no dual lane.
- **The float half of the Repeat test is `#[ignore]`d with its reason**, and reported as a failed gate. Left running, it would stop `./check.sh` on every Mac. Weakening it to the integer half alone would hide the finding.
- **The SIR bands run over the step from tick 0**, as `consistency_sir.rs` does, at its seed and one more.
- **A Repeat body's uniform values are hoisted, not refused.** The plan allows Repeat in every map pass and the catalogue allows these kinds in a body, so the compiler meets the file rather than narrowing it. The move happens in the host's program, so both backends run one schedule.
- **Pass memory is a `Demand` check against the device, not a compiler limit.** The compiler cannot see the device. The sizes are fields of `GridPassSpec`, zero when unknown as `cost` is, and `Demand::push_pass` returns the record so its two existing callers did not change.

## Issues found & future directions

- **Fast math on Metal.** A graph and a hand-written shader agree on one device when they write the same float expression, and a graph agrees with itself across runs. Two shapes of one float computation need not agree, and neither need the host. The plan's replay contract holds. Its Repeat gate, read as bit for bit on the device for any body, cannot hold under fast math. Three ways forward, for the maintainer: read the gate as the integer case plus replay; ask wgpu for `MTLMathModeSafe` (no option exists in wgpu 30, so this means a patch or an upstream feature); or have the emitter break contraction with an opaque operation, which costs every float node a few instructions and parts the graph from the stock ports. A2's contraction probe for `gpu_ants.toml` meets the same question, since the stock shader fuses `field * cut + reward`.
- **Vulkan and DX12.** The float test was run on Metal alone. Run it with `--ignored` on another backend before calling the gate met there.
- **The budget against Repeat.** At 16384 by 16384 a corpus file affords about 35 units a cell a step, so a Repeat of draws stops at 4 iterations there. A file with a larger bound builds at a smaller grid and is refused at the large one with a line naming the pass. The budget was measured on `gpu_sir`'s mix of work, and a body heavy in draws may run at another rate per unit.
- **Draw-dependent plants.** Changing `<` to `<=` against a draw is invisible to every test here, since a 24-bit draw never equals a rate of 32 bits. The known-answer cases of the comparison kinds hold that difference.
- **`schema_hash` and the model id.** The gate holds under the stock id, as decided. Nothing here changes it.
- **16 KiB of tallies.** A stats pass holds at most 4096 tallies in all, sixteen `stat.count_by` nodes over 256 variants. Splitting the tallies across several dispatches would lift that, at the cost of a reduce pass per 4096 counters.
- **Hand-written shaders declare no memory sizes.** A model type's uniform block and workgroup memory stay unchecked before a build, as its work does.
